Distilling L1 from an imitation teacher

L1's policy (a blind foot placement estimator, two terrain patches and a 90-muscle policy) never learned to walk from scratch. The new route: train an ordinary imitation teacher that sees the clip, then distil it into L1's two networks, which never see the clip. One clip first; expand once the student copies the teacher.

Teacher training on one clip (mc021, 1.2 m/s) Then: distil into the student Then: all of AB01, then stage 1

The plan

The teacher and the student run in the same simulation, so every state the student visits can be labelled with the teacher's action. The student's inputs are a subset of the teacher's: everything except the clip.

The student: foot placement estimator and muscle policy

L1's two networks, both at the clip's 40 Hz. The estimator guesses where each foot will land next; the terrain is read only at those two spots; the muscle policy turns everything into 90 muscle excitations. This is what the teacher is distilled into, and what stage 1 trains.

In the student the estimator is fixed: fitted to human footsteps (below) or to where the teacher's feet land. A critic (training only) also gets the noise-free state and the full 33 × 21 height map. During distillation the ground is flat or a straight ramp, and the patches read it.

The teacher

Ordinary imitation, the way STC trains this same body: the policy sees where the clip is going and is rewarded for following it. Run teacher_mc021.

SPRINT's recommended imitation terms; feet and body points relative to the pelvis in its heading frame, the pelvis by its height over the ground.

Inputs

L1's, plus the clip

Everything the student gets (body state, command, muscle states, two patches), plus the clip 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 14, 24 and 40 steps ahead (up to 1 s): joint angles, pelvis height, orientation, velocity and position, and the feet relative to the pelvis, each as the error from the body now.

Timing

The clip plays at its own rate

One clip frame per 40 Hz control step, the same rate as the policy and the estimator. Seeing the clip ahead, the teacher can stay in step on its own.

Episodes

Starts and falls

Starts from a random frame of the clip on flat ground (ramp clips on 5° or 10° ramp strips once all of AB01 is in). An episode ends when the pelvis is 15 cm off the clip's height or 60° off its orientation for 3 steps, when the clip ends, or after 10 s.

Learner

As before

SPRINT's PPO with the STC settings, 8,192 worlds; effort as STC weighs it (metabolic, cubed activation, compartment balance at 0.02 each); motor delays 10/20/40 ms.

Distillation into the student

Supervised, not reinforcement learning: the student learns to output the teacher's 90 excitations from L1's inputs alone.

DAgger

Labels on the states the student visits

Roll out a mix of teacher and student, moving toward the student; label every state with the teacher's action; fit the student to all labels so far. Copying only the teacher's own states would leave the student lost after its first mistake.

Estimator

Fixed during distillation

Either the estimator fitted to AB01's footsteps (held-out clips: about 5–7 cm median error while walking, 43 cm when starting, stopping or standing, against 57 cm for fixed spots) or one fitted to where the teacher's feet land. On flat ground the patches are constant, so for mc021 it does not matter.

Pass

Nearly the teacher

Within about 30 minutes of distillation the student tracks mc021 nearly as well as the teacher: most episodes finish the clip, joint error close to the teacher's. If it cannot, L1's inputs are missing something the teacher uses, and we find out what.

Why from scratch failed

No fixed muscle drive keeps this body up, and more drive only falls faster. An untrained policy is therefore rewarded for lowering its drive, and its calf, knee and hip extensors sank to about 0.02, a corner where learning can barely raise them again. Every from-scratch run collapsed within 2 s, whatever the effort weight, reward shape or training conditions.

Median time to fall from the training starts (level 0, delays on, no pushes), with every muscle held at one drive. Imitation avoids the problem: it pays for the right joint angles and pelvis height every step, so the policy never has to discover standing by surviving longer.

Stage 1 in detail

The B1 setup the from-scratch runs used, now starting from the walker.

Reward

Per control step

½ × speed × heading + ½ × style − effort. Speed exp(−2 × (error − 0.1)²), the first 0.1 m/s free; heading exp(−turn-rate error²); effort at fixed weights of 0.0025 each (metabolic power, summed cubed activation, compartment balance). A fall ends the episode with no penalty.

Style

The style judge

GAIT_amp's adversarial judge, trained alongside the policy: it learns to score human pose pairs +1 and the policy's −1, and the style reward is max(0, 1 − ¼ (score − 1)²). Each pair is two poses 25 ms apart: pelvis height over the ground, the pelvis's up direction, the 17 joint angles, both heels relative to the pelvis, and how far the pelvis moved and turned between them. Human pairs: 75,334 from AB01's 198 walking, start-stop and standing windows (all grades), mirrored half the time. It starts fresh in stage 1: the teacher and the student never use it.

Body

Body and physics

Rajagopal lower body: 90 muscles, 23 joints. 40 control steps a second, 75 physics steps each. Motor delays: 10 ms for hip and trunk muscles, 20 ms knee, 40 ms ankle and foot.

World

Ground

8 × 8 m tiles in 10 levels. Of the 10 columns, 6 are stepping stones (50 cm stones, 10 cm gaps over 30 cm holes at the easiest level), 3 slopes (up to 3° easiest, 29° hardest), 1 rough (2–10 cm bumps at every level). Worlds start at levels 0–2, go up after walking 4 m and down after less than half the commanded distance.

Senses

What the policy senses

Body state with sensor noise (joint speeds ±1.5 rad/s, body speed ±0.15 m/s), the command, five states per muscle, and 98 heights in two patches where the blind guesser expects the feet to land (±5 cm drift). The critic also gets the clean state and the full map.

Episodes

Starts and commands

A random frame of 152 level AB01 clips (walking, start-stop, standing; 28 % standing frames), with the clip's velocities. The command starts at that frame's speed and heading; every 10 s a new random speed (0–1.5 m/s) with the same heading: stage 1 walks straight. 20 s at most.

Disturbances

Pushes and falls

Friction ×0.5–2 per world, a constant push of up to 7 N per episode, a velocity kick every 4 s. A fall (tilt over 40°, pelvis under 0.5 m above the ground, or a thigh or shank touching) ends the episode with no penalty.

Learner

Learner

SPRINT's PPO with the STC settings: a 512-2048-512-2048-512 network, 8,192 worlds, 24 steps per update, 2 × 40 minibatches, a left-right symmetry loss. Starts from the distilled student's policy and estimator, with a fresh critic and optimiser. Whether stage 1 keeps the 0.05 drive floor of the from-scratch runs is still open.

Status and checks

Every step has a pass mark written down before it runs and a check within about an hour; a step that misses is stopped and diagnosed, not extended.

Running

Teacher on mc021

Run teacher_mc021, 150M steps, started 19 Nov. Check at about 50M steps (about 1 h).

Teacher passes if

It tracks the clip

By about 50M steps: most episodes finish the 10 s clip, joint error under about 6°, calf, knee and hip extensors working in stance. If not, the problem is in our environment (the method is proven) and we debug it there.

Then

Student, then expand

Distil on mc021 (pass: nearly the teacher). Only then: all of AB01 (276 windows, ramps included) for teacher and student, then stage 1 from the student.
