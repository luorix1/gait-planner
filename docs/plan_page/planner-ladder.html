Two-stage training for L1

Trained from scratch, L1's policy (Watch Your Step's blind foothold guesser and two terrain patches, on our 90-muscle body) never learned to bear weight. This test trains it in two stages: first it learns to walk by imitating AB01's clips, then it switches to the ladder's own reward.

Stage 0 running: b1_pretrain, 300M steps Keep-or-kill check at about 85M steps Stage 1 starts from stage 0's walker

The two stages

The policy's inputs are the same in both stages, so stage 1 picks up stage 0's weights unchanged. The clip appears only in stage 0's reward, never in the policy's input.

Stage 0

Imitation (run b1_pretrain)

Rewarded every step for matching an AB01 clip's joint angles, pelvis and foot positions, on flat ground. The policy gets only the clip's speed and heading, as its command. 300M steps.

Stage 1

The ladder's reward

Starts from stage 0's policy, critic and guesser. ½ speed × heading + ½ style − effort, on the terrain curriculum with delays, sensor noise and pushes.

Fair comparison

One walker for every rung

Every rung of the ladder will start from the same stage-0 walker, so differences between rungs still come from the rung.

Why from scratch failed

No fixed muscle drive keeps this body up, and more drive only falls faster. An untrained policy is therefore rewarded for lowering its drive, and its calf, knee and hip extensors sank to about 0.02, a corner where learning can barely raise them again. Every from-scratch run collapsed within 2 s, whatever the effort weight, reward shape or training conditions.

Median time to fall from the training starts (level 0, delays on, no pushes), with every muscle held at one drive. Imitation avoids the problem: it pays for the right joint angles and pelvis height every step, so the policy never has to discover standing by surviving longer.

Stage 0 in detail

SPRINT's recommended imitation terms and STC's effort and fall rule, adapted so the policy never sees the clip.

Most a step can earn: 1.0. Feet and body points are compared relative to the pelvis in its heading frame, and the pelvis by its height over the ground: with no clip in its input, the walker's position in the world drifts.

Inputs

What the policy sees

L1's inputs: body state, command, five states per muscle, two terrain patches (flat here). No reference clip.

Command and clock

Following the clip without seeing it

The command is the clip's speed (averaged over 1 s) and heading. The clip's clock follows the walker: each step it may slip or gain 2 frames toward the walker's pose, never more than 0.25 s from real time, so a slightly different step rhythm isn't punished and the walker can't stall it.

Episodes

Starts and falls

Starts from a random frame of AB01's 152 level windows (walking, start-stop, standing) on flat ground, with no pushes or friction spread. An episode ends when the pelvis is 15 cm off the clip's height or 60° off its orientation for 3 steps, when the clip ends, or after 10 s.

Effort and learner

As STC weighs it

Metabolic power, summed cubed activation and compartment balance at 0.02 each. SPRINT's PPO with the STC settings, no drive floor, every muscle starting at 0.05 drive.

Not yet

Left out of this run

AB01's 46 ramp windows (5° and 10°, up, down and standing): they need ramp ground, two straight ramp strips. No EMG reward (data/ab01 has no EMG). No pre-filled muscle activation at the start of an episode.

Check

A body on its clip scores 1

A test puts the body exactly on its reference frame: all seven terms score above 0.99, so frames, heading frames and body points line up.

Stage 1 in detail

The B1 setup the from-scratch runs used, now starting from the walker. Reward per control step: ½ × speed × heading + ½ × style − effort, with speed exp(−2 × (error − 0.1)²), the first 0.1 m/s free, heading exp(−turn-rate error²), and effort at fixed weights of 0.0025 each.

Body

Body and physics

Rajagopal lower body: 90 muscles, 23 joints. 40 control steps a second, 75 physics steps each. Motor delays: 10 ms for hip and trunk muscles, 20 ms knee, 40 ms ankle and foot.

World

Ground

8 × 8 m tiles in 10 levels. Of the 10 columns, 6 are stepping stones (50 cm stones, 10 cm gaps over 30 cm holes at the easiest level), 3 slopes (up to 3° easiest, 29° hardest), 1 rough (2–10 cm bumps at every level). Worlds start at levels 0–2, go up after walking 4 m and down after less than half the commanded distance.

Senses

What the policy senses

Body state with sensor noise (joint speeds ±1.5 rad/s, body speed ±0.15 m/s), the command, five states per muscle, and 98 heights in two patches where the blind guesser expects the feet to land (±5 cm drift). The critic also gets the clean state and the full map.

Episodes

Starts and commands

A random frame of 152 level AB01 clips (walking, start-stop, standing; 28 % standing frames), with the clip's velocities. The command starts at that frame's speed and heading; every 10 s a new random speed (0–1.5 m/s) and heading. 20 s at most.

Disturbances

Pushes and falls

Friction ×0.5–2 per world, a constant push of up to 7 N per episode, a velocity kick every 4 s. A fall (tilt over 40°, pelvis under 0.5 m above the ground, or a thigh or shank touching) ends the episode with no penalty.

Learner

Learner

SPRINT's PPO with the STC settings: a 512-2048-512-2048-512 network, 8,192 worlds, 24 steps per update, 2 × 40 minibatches, a left-right symmetry loss. Starts from stage 0's policy, critic and guesser with a fresh optimiser. Whether stage 1 keeps the 0.05 drive floor of the from-scratch runs is still open.

Status and the decision

Stage 0 runs to 300M steps (about 01:30). At about 85M steps it is checked against a rule fixed in advance.

Keep

Clearly beyond from scratch

Episodes well past the from-scratch runs' length at the same step count (about 57 steps at 85M), aiming for 100+ and rising; imitation reward climbing toward 0.6 or more and joint error toward 10°; calf, knee and hip extensor drive at or above the 0.05 start.

Kill

The same trap again

Episodes still under about 70 steps, and extensor drive sunk below 0.04.

Now

24M steps (20:21)

Episodes 40 steps (1 s), imitation reward 0.52 and rising, joint error 13° and falling. Metabolic power is falling (3.4 to 2.75 W/kg), the direction the failed runs took: the check decides.
