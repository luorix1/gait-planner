Teaching L1 to walk through an imitation teacher

The goal: a 90-muscle walker that follows speed and heading commands over uneven ground and moves like a person, from what it can sense alone. Trained from scratch it never stood up, so it is now trained in two phases: an imitation teacher that sees human clips, then DAgger to copy the teacher into the controller, which never sees them. Both phases pass on one clip, and the teacher passes on all of AB01, but the first distillation on all of AB01 fell short.

The goal

A muscle-driven walker that goes where it is told, over ground it has not walked on, and moves like a person, using only what a person has while walking: the command, its own body's senses and the ground near its feet. Human recordings are used in training only.

The controller: foot placement estimator and muscle policy

Its two networks learn at different times. The estimator is learned first, supervised: from each AB01 frame's body state and command it predicts where each foot actually landed next. It is then frozen, so the patches it places stay put while the muscle policy, the part that distillation and stage 1 train, learns to use them. L1's two networks, both at the clip's 40 Hz. The estimator guesses where each foot will land next; the terrain is read only at those two spots; the muscle policy turns everything into 90 muscle excitations. This is what the teacher is distilled into, and what stage 1 trains.

The estimator is fitted beforehand to AB01's human footsteps (on held-out clips: 5–7 cm median error while walking, 43 cm when starting, stopping or standing) and stays frozen in distillation. Only the muscle policy is trained. A critic (training only) also gets the noise-free state and the full 33 × 21 height map.

Two-phase training

Trained from scratch on the goal's reward, this body never learned to stand: no fixed muscle drive keeps it up, so an untrained policy is paid for doing less, and its leg muscles switch off. Imitation pays for the right pose at every step instead, so we first train a teacher that sees the clip, then copy it into the controller, which does not.

Phase 1 · training the teacher

SPRINT's port of STC's imitation reward and physics, trained with PPO and without the EMG term (AB01 has no EMG). STC itself trains this body with FlashSAC and adds error-weighted episode starts and reset priming; whether we need those is open (see Next).

One clip frame passes per 40 Hz control step. The clip ahead is read at 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 14, 24 and 40 steps (25 ms to 1 s). Episodes start at a random clip frame and end when the pelvis is 30 cm from the clip or 60° off for 3 steps, when the clip ends, or after 10 s.

The reward's terms to scale. Effort: metabolic power, cubed activation and compartment balance, 0.02 each.

Phase 1 · fitting the foot placement estimator

Plain supervised learning on the clips, no simulation and no reinforcement learning. For every frame of every AB01 window, the input is what the controller will sense at that moment, and the answer is where each foot really lands next. Done once, before distillation; then frozen.

84,582 labelled frames from AB01's 276 windows. The input is computed from the clip exactly as the simulation computes it at that moment (a test checks they agree), with the clip's own speed as the command. On 55 whole windows held out of the fit: 8.4 cm median error (5–7 cm while walking, 43 cm when starting, stopping or standing), against 57 cm for fixed foot spots. The saved estimator is then fitted on every window. Script fit_fpm.py.

Phase 2 · distilling into the student (DAgger)

Supervised, not reinforcement learning. One simulated body, two views of it: the teacher's inputs and L1's. Every state is labelled with the teacher's action, whoever is driving, and the student is fitted to every label so far. Because the student increasingly drives, it collects labels in the states its own mistakes lead to, which copying only the teacher's states would never show it.

β is 1 for the first 5 iterations (they also set the student's input normaliser), then falls linearly to 0 by iteration 85. Each iteration: 24 steps of all 4,096 worlds, then 64 gradient steps of 8,192 labels on the teacher's pre-squash mean. The estimator stays fixed, fitted to AB01's footsteps; on mc021's flat ground the patches read flat anyway. Script distill.py.

All of AB01: why the student failed, and the test now running

On all 276 AB01 windows the student copied the teacher badly (6.5°, 35 % falls, against 4.9° and 2 %). The teacher acts on the clip it follows; the student never sees the clip, so it learns the average of the teacher's actions over the clips, and the average falls. The test running now gives the student only the part of the clip a footstep planner could supply.
