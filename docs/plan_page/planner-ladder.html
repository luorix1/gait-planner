Distilling L1 from an imitation teacher

L1's policy (a blind foot placement estimator, two terrain patches and a 90-muscle policy) never learned to walk from scratch. The new route: train an ordinary imitation teacher that sees the clip, then distil it into L1's two networks, which never see the clip. On one clip (mc021) the student now copies the teacher nearly exactly; the teacher is training on all of AB01.

mc021: teacher and student passed Teacher training on all of AB01 (276 windows) Then: distil on AB01, then stage 1

The plan

The teacher and the student run in the same simulation, so every state the student visits can be labelled with the teacher's action. The student's inputs are a subset of the teacher's: everything except the clip.

The student: foot placement estimator and muscle policy

L1's two networks, both at the clip's 40 Hz. The estimator guesses where each foot will land next; the terrain is read only at those two spots; the muscle policy turns everything into 90 muscle excitations. This is what the teacher is distilled into, and what stage 1 trains.

In the student the estimator is fixed: fitted to human footsteps (below) or to where the teacher's feet land. A critic (training only) also gets the noise-free state and the full 33 × 21 height map. During distillation each clip walks on its own ground (flat or its ramp), and the patches read it.

The teacher

STC's imitation recipe, unchanged except for the EMG term (AB01 has no EMG): SPRINT's recommended reward, inputs, physics and PPO with its left-right symmetry loss, 4,096 worlds, motor delays of 10, 20 and 40 ms. It sees where the clip is going and is paid for following it.

The reward. Episodes start at a random frame with small noise and end when the pelvis is 30 cm from the clip or 60° off for 3 steps, when the clip ends, or after 10 s. Effort: metabolic power, cubed activation and compartment balance, 0.02 each.

Distillation into the student (DAgger)

Supervised, not reinforcement learning. One simulated body, two views of it: the teacher's inputs and L1's. Every state is labelled with the teacher's action, whoever is driving, and the student is fitted to every label so far. Because the student increasingly drives, it collects labels in the states its own mistakes lead to, which copying only the teacher's states would never show it.

β is 1 for the first 5 iterations (they also set the student's input normaliser), then falls linearly to 0 by iteration 85. Each iteration: 24 steps of all 4,096 worlds, then 64 gradient steps of 8,192 labels on the teacher's pre-squash mean. The estimator stays fixed, fitted to AB01's footsteps; on mc021's flat ground the patches read flat anyway. Script distill.py.

Why from scratch failed

No fixed muscle drive keeps this body up, and more drive only falls faster. An untrained policy is therefore rewarded for lowering its drive, and its calf, knee and hip extensors sank to about 0.02, a corner where learning can barely raise them again. Every from-scratch run collapsed within 2 s, whatever the effort weight, reward shape or training conditions.

Median time to fall from the training starts (level 0, delays on, no pushes), with every muscle held at one drive. Imitation avoids the problem: it pays for the right joint angles and pelvis height every step, so the policy never has to discover standing by surviving longer.

Stage 1 in detail

The B1 setup the from-scratch runs used, now starting from the distilled student: the clip is gone, the reward is the command plus a style judge.

RewardSpeed exp(−2 × (error − 0.1)²), the first 0.1 m/s free; heading exp(−turn-rate error²); style max(0, 1 − ¼ (score − 1)²); effort at fixed weights of 0.0025 each (metabolic power, summed cubed activation, compartment balance).
Style pairsTwo poses 25 ms apart: pelvis height over the ground, its up direction, the 17 joint angles, both heels relative to the pelvis, and how far the pelvis moved and turned. Human pairs from AB01's 198 walking, start-stop and standing windows (all grades), mirrored half the time. The judge starts fresh in stage 1.
Ground8 × 8 m tiles in 10 levels; of 10 columns, 6 stepping stones (50 cm stones, 10 cm gaps over 30 cm holes at the easiest level), 3 slopes (3° to 29°), 1 rough (2–10 cm bumps). Start at levels 0–2; up after walking 4 m, down after less than half the commanded distance.
EpisodesA random frame of 152 level AB01 clips (28 % standing frames) with its velocities; the command starts at that frame's speed and heading, and every 10 s a new speed (0–1.5 m/s), same heading. 20 s at most.
DisturbancesFriction ×0.5–2 per world, a constant push up to 7 N per episode, a velocity kick every 4 s. Falls: tilt over 40°, pelvis under 0.5 m above the ground, or a thigh or shank touching.
SensesL1's inputs with sensor noise (joint speeds ±1.5 rad/s, body speed ±0.15 m/s) and ±5 cm patch drift; the critic also gets the clean state and the full height map.
LearnerSPRINT's PPO with the STC settings, 8,192 worlds, 24 steps per update, symmetry loss; policy and estimator from the student, critic and optimiser fresh. Whether to keep the 0.05 drive floor is open.
